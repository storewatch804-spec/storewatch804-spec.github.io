France

Register
Registre national des producteurs (unique identifier: IDU — Identifiant Unique, formerly UIN), operated within SYDEREP; packaging EPR contributions collected via approved eco-organismes (Citeo, Léko, Adelphe)
Operator
ADEME (Agence de la transition écologique), under Art. L.541-10-13 Code de l'environnement; eco-organisme registration (e.g. Citeo) is the operational compliance route
Foreign seller must register
yes — mandatory since 1 Jan 2022 for all producers/distributors/importers placing packaging on the French market, EU or non-EU, no minimum volume; distance sellers into France are in scope
Authorised rep required
unclear/changing — as of today (13 Aug 2026) an authorised representative (mandataire) is described by compliance-industry sources as optional for EU-based producers but the EU PPWR makes an authorised representative MANDATORY for producers not established in the member state of first market placement, effective from 12 Aug 2026 (i.e., as of yesterday under this date) — official ADEME/government text confirming the exact French transposition date was not found in the sources fetched
Threshold
— (no de-minimis found; obligation applies from first unit placed on market per compliance-industry sources; no official ADEME statement of a threshold was located)
Fee band
IDU/register enrolment appears free; separate eco-organisme (Citeo) eco-contributions are per-kg material rates, e.g. ~€0.177–0.217/kg cardboard, ~€0.399–0.901/kg plastics (2026 Citeo barème, per AuditREP/Compliancr secondary sources) — no official flat 'registration fee' figure found on ademe.fr or citeo.com in the sources fetched
Deadline
IDU mandatory since 1 January 2022 (existing packaging EPR); PPWR-driven authorised-representative requirement from 12 August 2026 per secondary sources — could not verify official French government confirmation of this specific date in sources fetched

Germany

Register
LUCID Verpackungsregister (LUCID Packaging Register)
Operator
Zentrale Stelle Verpackungsregister (ZSVR) — Central Agency Packaging Register
Foreign seller must register
yes — every company, anywhere in the world, that places packaged goods on the German market commercially (including online/distance sellers) must register in LUCID; no minimum quantity or revenue threshold
Authorised rep required
yes, for foreign sellers without a German establishment — a foreign company selling packaging/packaged goods directly to German end users without a local branch must appoint an authorised representative (Bevollmächtigter) who assumes the EPR obligations on its behalf, per verpackungsregister.org (official ZSVR site)
Threshold
— (no de-minimis; official page states no minimum quantities or sales limits)
Fee band
LUCID registration itself: free of charge (official, verpackungsregister.org). Separate mandatory dual-system packaging-licence fee (system participation) is volume/material-dependent — secondary sources cite marketplace-seller entry tariffs 'from €23.89/year' and 'under €100/year' for small sellers, but no official ZSVR figure for this variable fee was found (it is set by competing licensing companies, not ZSVR)
Deadline
VerpackG (Packaging Act) in force since 1 January 2019, LUCID mandatory since then for the pre-existing national EPR regime; PPWR (EU-wide) enters into force 12 August 2026, per verpackungsregister.org, with an ongoing duty to keep LUCID registration data current through the transition — no separate new German registration deadline distinct from the EU-wide PPWR date was stated on the page fetched

Netherlands

Register
No separate government packaging register — producer obligations are administered directly through the PRO, Verpact (formerly Afvalfonds Verpakkingen / Stichting Verpakkingenafvalfonds)
Operator
Verpact (the Dutch packaging waste fund / producer responsibility organisation)
Foreign seller must register
yes, if above the threshold — business.gov.nl (official Dutch government portal) states foreign companies selling directly to Dutch consumers online must comply with the packaging waste management contribution obligation once they exceed the 50,000 kg/year threshold
Authorised rep required
unclear/qualified — sources agree a Dutch-based EPR authorised representative becomes mandatory for all non-established producers only once the EU PPWR provision takes effect on 12 August 2026; before that date, secondary sources say a Dutch authorised representative is currently mandatory only for single-use-plastic packaging sold cross-border by a non-resident producer, not for general packaging. This PPWR-vs-pre-existing-regime distinction was reported by secondary sources (Deutsche Recycling, RegSurance); not independently confirmed on business.gov.nl or verpact.nl
Threshold
50,000 kg of packaging material placed on the Dutch market per calendar year (all materials combined, not per material) — confirmed on business.gov.nl (official). Note: 0 kg threshold (no exemption) applies to Single-Use Plastics (SUP)-covered packaging and Deposit Return System (Statiegeld) items regardless of overall volume, per secondary sources — not independently confirmed on an official page
Fee band
Verpact 'system fee' for reusable packaging: EUR 0.015 per kg (stated for 2024 on verpact.nl/en/rates); general material-specific waste management contribution rates are published on Verpact's rates page but exact current per-material figures were not extracted from the fetched content — reported as "—" beyond the reusable-packaging system fee found
Deadline
Annual report on prevention/recycling measures due before 1 August each year (business.gov.nl, official); separate tonnage declaration to Verpact due by 31 March of the following year (per secondary source, not independently confirmed on an official page); PPWR mandatory-authorised-representative rule takes effect 12 August 2026
